About the founder

Meet Zeal.

Zeal Thakkar

Hi, I’m Zeal Thakkar, a passionate graphic designer, video editor, and social media manager with over 10 years of experience in the creative industry. My journey started with a love for design and storytelling, which I turned into a thriving career. From playing badminton to crafting calligraphy and resin art, my creative spirit is always active, fueling my vision to help brands stand out in a competitive world.

With a commitment to innovation and attention to detail, I’m dedicated to delivering designs that leave a lasting impression. I hold a DAFM from MAAC, a Diploma in Graphic Designing from Arena Animations, and a Bachelor’s degree in Management from the University of Mumbai.

About DesignO'Hive

A hive built
for good ideas.

At Design O’Hive, we believe in the power of creativity to transform ideas into impactful designs. As a growing design agency, we specialize in tailor-made solutions that resonate with your audience and elevate your brand—from eye-catching graphics and compelling videos to strategic social media management.

Design O’Hive represents a buzzing hive of creativity and collaboration, where innovative ideas come together to shape unique brand identities. Like a hive that thrives with teamwork, we co-create with our clients to bring their visions to life.
